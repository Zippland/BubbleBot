End the current turn without sending any reply to the user or channel.

Call this with no arguments when silence is genuinely the best response: for example, a group message matched your name incidentally ("泡泡玛特" / "泡泡浴"), casual chatter is not addressed to you, a periodic condition was not met, or the user explicitly asked for no reply.

Call it immediately once you decide to stay silent, preferably as the first and only tool call. It cannot undo messages, progress updates, or side effects that were already produced. Do not use it merely to avoid answering a clear request that is addressed to you.
